I am a first-year PhD student in Computer Vision at the University of Bristol. I completed my Master's at Sharif University of Technology (SUT), <a href='http://ipl.ce.sharif.edu/'>Image Processing Lab (IPL)</a>, under the supervision of <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mvx4PvgAAAAJ&hl=en'>Prof. Kasaei</a>. In the summer of 2022, I was a Research Intern at the <a href='ispgroup.gitlab.io'>Image and Signal Processing Group</a>, UCLouvain, under the supervision of <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=xb3Zc3cAAAAJ&hl=en'>Prof. De Vleeschouwer</a>. Before joining SUT, I completed my Bachelor's in Computer Engineering at <a href='https://english.iut.ac.ir/'>Isfahan University of Technology (IUT)</a> under the supervision of <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=mZGNr2QAAAAJ'>Prof. Karimi</a>.


My research interest include: Sport Video Analysis, Model Compression via Knowledge Distillation, and Vision-Language Models for Document Analysis.
